LISSA privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

LISSA is a personal assistant that runs only on its owner's own laptop. It is used by its owner and is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data LISSA uses

  • Google Calendar: to list and add the owner's events and remind them of meetings.
  • Google Tasks: to list, add and complete the owner's tasks.
  • Google Contacts (read-only): names and phone numbers, so the owner can message a person by name.
  • Gmail (read-only, only for accounts the owner chooses): to summarise unread mail for the owner.

Where the data goes

The data is processed on the owner's laptop by software running there. It is not sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's laptop.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the data LISSA keeps can be deleted from the laptop.

LISSA's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
